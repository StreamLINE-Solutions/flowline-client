FlowLINE — Remote assistance for SMEs.

FlowLINE lets your IT technician take control of your screen (with your consent) to help you remotely.

• Remote screen control (mouse, touch, keyboard)
• File transfer
• End-to-end encrypted sessions
• Hosted in Switzerland, no telemetry

To be controlled remotely from another device, FlowLINE requires the Android "Accessibility" permission (AccessibilityService API). It is used only for remote control.

Need help? Contact your company's IT service.
